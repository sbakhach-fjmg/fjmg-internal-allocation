How the numbers are built

Data

Advent sales export, sales sheet. Used · Retail or Lease · active stores only. Wholesale deals and former stores are dropped. Deals are keyed on store + deal number + VIN so monthly re-uploads merge; the analysis uses the last {{ANALYSIS_MONTHS}} months ending at the newest sale.

Spec

Every VIN is decoded once with MarketCheck NeoVIN and cached: model, trim, body, drivetrain, powertrain, exterior color, MSRP. Year and make come from Advent. Mileage bands: 0-10k · 10-20k · 20-35k · 35-50k · 50-75k · 75-100k · 100k+. Spec = drivetrain · powertrain.

Matching (Placement)

A VIN is matched as closely as possible — year, make, model, trim, spec, mileage band and factory options — then one thing is relaxed at a time until a level has at least the cohort minimum of deals. Options match when a sold unit carries every key package family the car has (AMG Line, Night, Premium, Driver Assistance, Exclusive, Pinnacle, M Sport, Shadowline, Executive, Black Optic, S Sport, Sport Chrono, TRD, F Sport, Technology); a car with only minor packages needs half of them matched. Options are kept while miles, spec and year are relaxed, then dropped. Order:

Per-store figures

Avg front / back / total — plain averages of the store's deals in the cohort. Total = front + back.

Adj — the same average shrunk toward the cohort-wide mean: (n × store avg + K × cohort avg) / (n + K). With K = {{logic.k|int}}, a store with {{logic.k|int}} deals is weighted half on its own numbers; with 3 deals mostly on the group's.

Med days / Adj days — median and shrunk average of receive → sold days (clipped to 1–365).

Gross / slot / yr — adj total × 365 / adj days (days floored at {{MIN_DAYS}}).

Similar — for the vehicle being placed, each of the store's sold units scores 0–1: half from matching spec attributes (trim, drivetrain, powertrain, body, mileage band, color, year) and half from the share of the car's factory packages the unit also carries. The store's figure is the sum, i.e. a count of look-alike units. Options match is that package share on its own, averaged over the store's units.

Ranked / thin — a store needs {{logic.min_store}} deals in the cohort to be ranked; thinner stores are listed but greyed out.
